BRAND LOGOS DIRECTORY
======================

Place official brand logos in this folder.
Logos will automatically be rendered on brand cards across the catalog and brands pages.

Image Specifications:
---------------------
- Formats: Vector SVG (.svg) preferred, PNG (.png) with transparent background accepted.
- Dimensions: At least 300 px wide.
- Centered, preserving aspect ratio.
- Official logos only.
