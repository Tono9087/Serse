CLIENT LOGOS DIRECTORY
======================

Place official client logos in this folder.
Logos will be displayed in the "Empresas que confían en nosotros" section on the Home page.

Expected File Names:
--------------------
- harman.png
- american-industries.png
- bwi-group.png

Image Specifications:
---------------------
- Transparent background (PNG or SVG).
- Width: At least 300px wide.
- Centered, preserving aspect ratio.
- Official client logos only.
